Boundary geometry dated 2 August 2022; valid_on 16 August 2022.
339 DS divisions in this version. Later administrative changes may be missing.

Changes by OpenATG: retained DS geometry without coordinate simplification or
rounding; divided features into district files; reduced properties; created a
trilingual name catalogue and bounding boxes. Warning names are separately
mapped to administrative codes using explicit aliases. These administrative
boundaries are not flood extents, landslide polygons or household risk maps.

Warnings and selected water observations are attributed to the Sri Lanka
Irrigation Department, DMC, NBRI and Department of Meteorology. Original source
links and SHA-256 hashes appear in data/provenance.json. No official partnership
or comprehensive/realtime coverage is claimed.

Display basemap adaptation (release 20261026-5): map-overview.json is separately
simplified at a 0.004-degree display tolerance and rounded to five decimals.
It is for drawing only. Exact district geometry remains the matching source.

Reservoir point locations: Sri Lanka Irrigation Department ICT/GIS,
Scheme_Data_2, layer 3. Source URL, retrieval time and hash are recorded in
data/reservoir-locations.json. Unique exact-name joins only; ambiguous and
unmatched records are omitted. Points do not establish upstream connectivity.

Sources, freshness & what this tool cannot tell you

FloodLens is an independent OpenATG awareness tool. It is not a validated forecasting system or an official emergency service. No flood depth, arrival time or safe-route prediction is provided.

River connections, catchments, terrain, releases, rainfall and tides all matter. Straight-line distance cannot estimate flood arrival. Historical and satellite evidence is not a current local warning.

Bulletins are reviewed snapshots. Automatic server-side bulletin publishing is not enabled. River records can be refreshed while the page is open. A successful download does not make an old observation fresh.

Interface translations are provided for access; they are not official translations. Read the original bulletin in your language. Independent native-speaker safety review is pending.

# Future community-report boundary — not connected

Telegram, Grok and social-media inputs are not connected in this release. No
private channel, unidentified agent or social account is accessed. This is a
future contract, not a source of current warnings.

A separate review queue should accept a stable report ID, source platform and
public source URL (or explicitly authorized private-source reference), original
report time and time zone, ingestion time, stated location and its uncertainty,
original content hash, consent/usage basis, and corroboration state. Missing
time, location or origin must remain unknown. Remove personal contact details,
faces and precise household locations from any public presentation unless
publication is separately authorized.

Keep these states distinct: unverified report, independently corroborated
report, and report linked to an identified official bulletin. Preserve who or
what reviewed the evidence, source dependencies, contradictory reports,
retractions and expiry. Two copied posts are not two independent sources.
Social-media popularity is not corroboration.

Community observations must never modify reviewed official warning severity,
DS coverage, validity, evacuation instructions or a location's danger level.
No social post alone may become a Critical or Red result. If eventually shown,
community reports belong in a separate, clearly labelled Details layer with
source time, uncertainty and corroboration state. Unknown is retained when no
validated location result exists. No autonomous warning interpretation, routing,
arrival time or depth prediction is authorized by this contract.

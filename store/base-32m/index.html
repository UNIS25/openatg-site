Research checkpoint

ATG Base 32M

An independently pretrained compact generative language-model research checkpoint exploring local and accessible AI for English, Sinhala and Tamil.

Research checkpoint. Not production-ready.

OpenATG research

32M Lightning v2

Checkpoint overview

A compact foundation for controlled research.

Demonstrated limitations

Controlled evaluation has not demonstrated reliable general-purpose conversation, translation, summarisation, email drafting or structured task completion. Outputs may be repetitive, irrelevant, incorrect or fabricated.

Do not rely on this checkpoint for medical, legal, financial, humanitarian, security, eligibility or other consequential decisions.
